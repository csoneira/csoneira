## Research Profile

My research focuses on the design, commissioning, and performance characterization of radiation detectors in accelerator and non-accelerator contexts. The two main axes of my recent work are:

- **Cosmic-ray instrumentation:** development and validation of RPC/MRPC detector systems within the miniTRASGO line.
- **Medical physics instrumentation:** detector-related developments for PET-based workflows and proton-therapy verification environments.

This combination has allowed me to gain experience in detector operation, calibration, timing/efficiency studies, experimental data analysis, and interdisciplinary collaboration.
