Dear Selection Committee,

I am writing in strong support of Mr. Cayetano Soneira Landín and his application to the ESA-FAIR Radiation Summer School 2026.

Cayetano is currently a predoctoral researcher at Universidad Complutense de Madrid, where he is developing a solid research profile in nuclear and particle physics instrumentation. He combines a strong academic foundation (double degree in Physics and Mathematics, followed by a master’s degree in Particle Physics and the Cosmos) with practical experience in detector development and data analysis.

His recent work includes contributions to RPC/MRPC detector systems for cosmic-ray studies and to detector-oriented research in medical imaging and in-beam PET for proton-therapy contexts. He has already demonstrated the ability to transform experimental work into scientific outputs, including peer-reviewed publications and oral presentations at specialized conferences.

From my perspective, one of Cayetano’s strongest qualities is his capacity to integrate theoretical understanding with experimental implementation. He approaches problems with maturity, works reliably in collaborative environments, and shows clear motivation to deepen his knowledge in radiation physics. These are precisely the attributes needed to benefit from, and contribute to, a demanding school such as ESA-FAIR.

I am convinced that participation in this program will significantly strengthen his doctoral training, and I fully recommend him for admission.
