Dear Selection Committee,

I am pleased to strongly support the application of Mr. Cayetano Soneira Landín to the ESA-FAIR Radiation Summer School 2026.

Cayetano is a predoctoral researcher at the Universidad Complutense de Madrid within the Nuclear Physics Group. I know his work in detector instrumentation and data analysis, where he has consistently shown technical rigor, autonomy, and excellent scientific attitude. This combination of instrumentation expertise, accelerator-based experimental exposure, and strong quantitative training makes him an excellent fit for the scope of your program.

In addition to his technical capabilities, Cayetano collaborates effectively in interdisciplinary environments and communicates clearly. He has demonstrated this through conference presentations, teamwork in experimental campaigns, and mentoring activities in detector training settings. I am confident he will contribute actively to both the theoretical and practical components of the ESA-FAIR school.
