Dear Selection Committee,

I am writing to apply for the ESA-FAIR Radiation Summer School 2026 (16 August - 1 September 2026, Darmstadt). I am a predoctoral researcher and PhD student at the Universidad Complutense de Madrid (UCM), working in the Nuclear Physics Group on radiation detector instrumentation for cosmic-ray studies and medical applications. The school is directly aligned with my research path and with the skills I need to strengthen during my PhD.

My current work combines two complementary lines. First, I contribute to the development and characterization of RPC/MRPC detectors for cosmic-ray monitoring, including commissioning, timing/efficiency studies, and data analysis in the miniTRASGO project. This work has resulted in first-author peer-reviewed publications in *Nuclear Instruments and Methods A* and *Advances in Space Research*. Second, within the NeWMBI environment at UCM, I participate in detector-related studies for medical imaging and in-beam PET applications connected to proton-therapy verification, which has given me practical exposure to radiation measurements in accelerator-based settings.

The ESA-FAIR school offers a rare combination of topics that I consider essential for my development: space radiation physics and biology, particle accelerators, and practical training at GSI/FAIR. I am particularly interested in learning how current space-radiation risk questions are addressed experimentally and how heavy-ion facilities can be used to produce robust data for both space and terrestrial applications. The lectures and laboratory activities would provide highly valuable training that is difficult to access in a single program elsewhere.

I also value the collaborative format of the school. I have worked in multidisciplinary environments and participated in detector schools and international experiments, including a campaign at JYFL-ACCLAB in Finland and tutoring activities at CERN DRD1. I am confident I can contribute actively to teamwork assignments and discussions while learning from participants with diverse backgrounds in physics, biology, and medicine.

Participation in the ESA-FAIR Radiation Summer School would significantly reinforce my doctoral training and help me connect my current detector work with broader questions in space-radiation research. I am strongly motivated to attend and to apply the acquired knowledge to ongoing and future projects at UCM.
