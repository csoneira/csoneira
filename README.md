# Cayetano Soneira-Landín

Researcher in **Nuclear and Particle Physics** at the **Complutense University of Madrid**. I work on radiation detector development, cosmic-ray instrumentation and medical imaging applications.

This repository compiles code, publications and other material related to my scientific activity.

---

## Research Interests

- Radiation detectors and electronic instrumentation
- Cosmic-ray monitoring with Resistive Plate Chambers (RPCs)
- Medical imaging and proton-therapy dosimetry

---
