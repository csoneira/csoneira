# Cayetano Soneira-Landín

Predoctoral researcher in Nuclear and Particle Physics at the Complutense University of Madrid, working on radiation detectors, cosmic-ray instrumentation, and medical imaging.

## Research

- Radiation detector instrumentation
- Resistive Plate Chambers and cosmic-ray monitoring
- Medical imaging and proton-therapy dosimetry

## Selected publications

- *miniTRASGO: Design and Initial Results of a Compact Resistive Plate Chamber Telescope for Worldwide Cosmic Ray Monitoring*, Advances in Space Research (2025). [DOI 10.1016/j.asr.2025.07.096](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.asr.2025.07.096)
- *Real-time Dose Reconstruction in Proton Therapy from In-beam PET Measurements*, Physics in Medicine & Biology (2025). [DOI 10.1088/1361-6560/adbfd9](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6560/adbfd9)
- *Deployment of a MiniTrasgo Detector at UDEM*, Proceedings of Science, PoS(ICRC2025)329 (2025)
- *miniTRASGO: a compact RPC telescope for global cosmic ray monitoring*, Proceedings of Science, PoS(ICRC2025)1368 (2025)
